Room-by-room decor ideas for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and more. Practical, renter-friendly, and made for real homes. This page points to three of our favourite guides on Decor With Purpose.

A bedroom that feels calm, not stark

Dark green and black sound bold for a bedroom, but paired with warm wood, soft bedding and plenty of natural light they make the room feel settled rather than heavy. The trick is balance: one dark wall or dark bedding, then lighter textures everywhere else so the room still feels bright in the morning. Our guide to black and green bedroom ideas walks through fifteen ways to get that balance right, from paint and bedding to lighting and small storage touches that keep the room tidy.

A backyard pool that feels like a retreat

An above-ground pool does not have to look temporary. A simple deck, a few planters, outdoor lighting and a place to sit turn the space around it into somewhere you actually want to spend an evening. Start with shade and seating, then add the details that make it feel finished rather than half done. Our above-ground pool ideas cover decks, landscaping, lighting and privacy, so the pool area feels like part of the garden instead of an afterthought.

A boho living room that feels lived in

Boho works best when it is layered, not bought in one go. Mix textures — a woven rug, linen cushions, wood, rattan — and let the room build up over time with pieces you actually like. Keep the colours warm and the layout easy to sit in, and the room will feel cosy rather than cluttered. Our guide to boho living room decor ideas has fifteen ideas for layering colour, texture and light in a way that still leaves room to move.
